# News Research Skill

## Goal
Find recent, relevant news and official updates related to the user's question.

## Procedure
1. Prioritize primary sources: public health agencies, government departments, WHO, peer-reviewed publications, and official announcements.
2. Check the publication date and the date of the event. Do not describe old information as breaking or current.
3. Include the source name, date when available, and direct URL for important claims.
4. Separate confirmed facts from reported claims, analysis, and uncertainty.
5. If search results are missing, stale, or inaccessible, say so. Never invent articles, dates, quotes, or URLs.
6. Summarize only what is relevant to the question.
