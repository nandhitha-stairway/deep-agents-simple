# Preventive Care Skill

## Goal
Summarize evidence-based prevention and risk-reduction guidance relevant to the question.

## Procedure
1. Prefer current guidance from official health authorities and reputable clinical/public-health organizations.
2. Consider relevant topics such as vaccination, ventilation, hand hygiene, masks, testing, staying home when ill, and protecting high-risk people when applicable.
3. Explain who the guidance applies to and note when recommendations vary by age, location, health status, or current risk.
4. Distinguish official recommendations from general information.
5. Do not diagnose individuals or provide personalized treatment instructions.
6. Cite source names, dates when available, and URLs. If evidence is missing or outdated, state the limitation.
