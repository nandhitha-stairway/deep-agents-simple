# Communicable Disease Surveillance Skill

## Goal
Assess available signals about communicable disease activity without overstating what the data can show.

## Procedure
1. Prefer official surveillance dashboards, public-health agency reports, laboratory/epidemiology publications, and wastewater surveillance sources where relevant.
2. Look for trends over time, geographic scope, case/test positivity, hospitalizations, deaths, variants, and wastewater signals when available and relevant.
3. Always note the data's reporting period, geography, and limitations.
4. Do not compare numbers from different methods or regions as if directly equivalent.
5. Distinguish observed surveillance signals from forecasts or speculation.
6. Cite source names, dates, and URLs. If current data cannot be retrieved, explicitly say so.
